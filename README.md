# Guitar-Pedals

## Tip-Ring-Sleeve (TRS) vs Tip-Sleeve (TS)

Guitar pedals usually use TS calbes for signal to connect from pedal to pedal or from guitar to pedal to the amp but TRS typically has a different use either used in conjunction with a separate foot pedal that controls a switch on the pedal or is used to send MIDI signal from on place to another

- TRS = balanced signal
   - Tip
      - Hot Signal (+)
   - Ring
      - Cold Signal (-)
   - Sleeve
      - Ground
- TS = unbalance signal
   - Tip
      - Signal Wire (Input)
   - Sleeve
      - Ground

## Buffers vs True By pass

Buffers and true bypass are two methods used in guitar pedals to manage signal integrity. Buffers provide high input impedance and low output impedance to maintain signal strength and quality over long cables or multiple pedals, preventing tone loss. True bypass, on the other hand, completely removes the pedal’s circuitry from the signal path when the pedal is off, ensuring the signal remains unaltered but potentially suffers from signal degradation in longer setups. Guitarists often use a combination of both to balance signal preservation and tonal purity, with buffers placed at strategic points in the signal chain to prevent loss and maintain consistency.
